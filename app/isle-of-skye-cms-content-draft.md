## faqs (array — 8 entries)

**Q:** How long is the Isle of Skye tour from Inverness?
**A:** The tour runs approximately 10-12 hours door-to-door. We typically depart around 8 AM and return by 7-8 PM, depending on stops and your preferences.

**Q:** Is this a private tour or a group bus tour?
**A:** This is a fully private tour for your party only. You travel in a comfortable vehicle with your own driver-guide — no strangers, no fixed schedule, no rushing between stops.

**Q:** Can we customise the itinerary?
**A:** Absolutely. The route above is our recommended itinerary, but you can skip stops, linger longer at favourites, or add alternatives like the Fairy Glen or Dunvegan Castle. Just let us know your interests.

**Q:** What's included in the price?
**A:** Door-to-door pickup and drop-off in Inverness, private vehicle, driver-guide, bottled water, and all fuel/parking. Castle entry fees and meals are at your own expense.

**Q:** How many people fit in one vehicle?
**A:** Our standard vehicle seats up to 7 passengers comfortably. For larger groups we can arrange multiple vehicles — contact us for a quote.

**Q:** What should I wear?
**A:** Scottish weather changes quickly. Bring layers, a waterproof jacket, and sturdy walking shoes or boots. Trainers are fine for roadside viewpoints but not for Fairy Pools.

**Q:** Is the tour suitable for children?
**A:** Yes. Families with kids of all ages join us regularly. We adjust walk lengths and stop times to suit your group.

**Q:** What happens if the weather is bad?
**A:** Skye is stunning in all weather — mist adds atmosphere! If conditions make a particular walk unsafe, we swap to a sheltered alternative like the Fairy Glen or a cosy cafe stop.

## whyChooseUs (richText — paste as rich text)

### Why Choose a Private Isle of Skye Tour?

Many visitors to the Scottish Highlands face a choice: join a large coach tour or book a private experience. Here's why a private tour delivers better value for your holiday.

**Your Pace, Not a Schedule**
Bus tours follow fixed timetables. If you want five more minutes at the Old Man of Storr, tough luck — the group is leaving. On a private tour, you decide when to linger and when to move on. Want to wait for the clouds to lift before that perfect photo? We'll wait with you.

**Small Groups Mean Better Access**
Coach tours can carry 50+ passengers. Imagine 50 people arriving at the Fairy Pools car park at once. With a private tour, you avoid the crowds. We know the quiet times and alternative viewpoints that big operators can't reach.

**Flexible Stops & Hidden Gems**
We can detour to spots that buses simply can't access: single-track roads to secluded beaches, a local croft for Highland cattle photos, or a waterfall that isn't in any guidebook. Your driver-guide knows dozens of these — just ask.

**Door-to-Door Convenience**
No meeting points, no waiting for latecomers, no walking to a coach station. We collect you from your hotel, B&B, or Airbnb in Inverness and return you to the same spot. Your holiday starts the moment you step outside.

**Personal Service, Real Connection**
Every tour has one dedicated driver-guide — yours for the whole day. No script, no rotating guides reciting memorised facts. Your guide will share local knowledge, point out things you'd miss from a coach window, and stop when you spot something interesting. You get a day on Skye, not a guided lecture.
